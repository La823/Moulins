Our Team

Meet the dedicated professionals behind our mission to deliver exceptional solutions and innovation.

Pardeep Arora

Director, Sales and Marketing, Moulins Pharmaceuticals

With over 33 years of experience in pharmaceutical sales and marketing, Pardeep Arora chose to step away from a successful corporate career to make a greater impact in healthcare. As the founder of Metamorf Lifesciences, he led the company to touch the lives of 1 crore patients per annum, driven by the vision of "Transforming Lives to Transform the Lives of Healthcare Partners." Now, as Director of Sales and Marketing at Moulins Pharmaceuticals, Pardeep brings his extensive expertise and strategic leadership to drive growth and expand access to quality healthcare. His commitment to "Healthcare, Beyond Medicine" ensures that Moulins continues to prioritize care, compassion, and meaningful change in the healthcare industry.

Tanmay Arora

CEO, Moulins Pharmaceuticals

An accomplished chemical engineer from IIT Kharagpur, Tanmay Arora brings a wealth of experience to Moulins Pharmaceuticals. He was part of the prestigious Young Leaders Program at Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, gaining extensive expertise across both research and manufacturing domains. As Excellence Head, he led transformative initiatives that enhanced operational efficiency and innovation. Now, as the CEO of Moulins Pharmaceuticals, Tanmay is committed to driving the company's mission of "Healthcare, Beyond Medicine." His leadership focuses on delivering impactful healthcare solutions, fostering innovation, and ensuring compassionate care reaches those who need it most.

Durgesh Saini

Director, Sales, Moulins Pharmaceuticals

With over 15 years of experience in pharmaceutical sales, Durgesh Saini has built a reputation for driving growth and building strong market presence. As the General Manager at Metamorf Lifesciences, he played a pivotal role in expanding the company’s reach and touching the lives of countless patients. Now, as Director of Sales at Moulins Pharmaceuticals, Durgesh brings his strategic expertise and deep market understanding to lead the sales team. His commitment to "Healthcare, Beyond Medicine" ensures that Moulins continues to provide accessible, quality healthcare solutions to communities everywhere.
